  1. Start on a textured floor with cracks, gravel, grass or a patterned rug. Keep tables and other horizontal surfaces out of view.
  2. Press Start AR, allow camera and motion access, then move the phone slowly until tracking is normal.
  3. Tap the floor to place the island. Sidestep or walk around to check that it stays put.

Placement uses 8th Wall's one estimated floor plane (Y = 0), not ARKit tabletop detection. A table is exploratory only and may confuse tracking. Your card can stay out of view; this page does not use the illustration. The island is sized for a phone held a step or two away — step back if the framing feels tight.
