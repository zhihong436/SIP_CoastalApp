Coastal AR

Point the rear camera at the printed coastal illustration. A miniature animated sea, beach and breakwater will sit on the card in perspective.

  1. Print the A5 card, or show it on a second screen.
  2. Allow the rear camera when the browser asks.
  3. Hold the card flat in good light and fill the frame with the illustration.
  4. Tap a glowing ring to place a breakwater section broadside to the waves, then rotate it 90° to turn it edge-on and compare the water behind it.

Camera frames are processed on this device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

What this shows — and what it does not

Waves roll in from the open sea. A breakwater section placed broadside to them casts a simple wave shadow: the water behind that section flattens while the water in front stays rough. Rotating it 90° turns it edge-on, so its added shelter drops away. The island's own lee is the baseline; the player-built shadow is separate.

This is a simplified illustration, not an engineering simulation. Breakwaters reduce wave energy; they do not prevent higher sea levels from flooding land.

This is a standalone feasibility experiment, not the paired trail game and not a finished activity.
