Phone test instructions
  1. A phone cannot open a QR that points to localhost. Print a card only after the URL field contains an address the phone can reach.
  2. The simplest remote test is an HTTPS preview URL on an approved host. Open it on the phone once, scan the printed QR, allow the camera, and point the rear camera at the illustration.
  3. A trusted local HTTPS setup also works if the phone can reach that host on the same network. Plain HTTP camera access is blocked by Safari except on localhost, which is not the phone.
  4. Use A5 paper at 100%, or A4 with "fit to page". The illustration should be roughly 110–125 mm square and printed in colour. Matte paper reduces glare.
  5. Build and desktop checks do not prove Safari camera or tracking behaviour. Record the real-iPhone result separately.

Coastal AR

A miniature sea, beach and breakwater that sits on this card.

1. Scan the QR code to open the experiment.

2. Allow the rear camera.

3. Point the camera at the illustration, not the QR code. Hold the card flat and fill the frame.

4. Tap a glowing ring to place a breakwater section broadside to the waves. Use Rotate 90° to turn it edge-on and compare the water behind it.

Illustrative only: broadside sections add a local wave shadow behind them; rotating 90° removes it. Breakwaters reduce wave energy; they do not prevent higher sea levels from flooding land.
