Phone test instructions
  1. Both players mark ready in the lobby first. Each phone then needs its own scan or testing entry; one player's scan does not open the stop on the other phone.
  2. A phone cannot open a QR that points to localhost. Print a card only after the URL field contains an address the phone can reach.
  3. The simplest remote test is an HTTPS preview URL on an approved host. Open it on the phone once, scan the printed QR, allow camera and motion access, then tap a textured floor patch to place the coast.
  4. A trusted local HTTPS setup also works if the phone can reach that host on the same network. Plain HTTP camera access is blocked by Safari except on localhost, which is not the phone.
  5. The illustration is decorative, not a tracking target. Use a floor with cracks, gravel, grass or a patterned rug, and keep tables and other horizontal surfaces out of view. Use A5 paper at 100%, or A4 with "fit to page"; print in colour and use matte paper to reduce glare.
  6. Build and desktop checks do not prove Safari camera or world-tracking behaviour. Record the real-iPhone result separately.

Coastal protection · paired hunt

Break the waves

Two phones, one shared coast. Place it on a textured floor, then build your own breakwater pieces.

1. Both players start the trail together and mark ready in the lobby.

2. Each player scans this QR with the phone camera or the in-app scanner.

3. Tap Start camera, then tap a textured floor patch once to place the coast. The illustration is decorative, not a tracking target.

4. Each player places and rotates their own two breakwater pieces on the water. Both phones show the shared accepted build.

Stop 1 of the hunt. The next clue, Higher ground, is not built yet. The shelter model is illustrative: breakwaters reduce wave energy, but they do not stop flooding from higher sea levels.
